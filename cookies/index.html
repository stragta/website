Cookies

Cookies and local storage

Last updated: 20 September 2026

No consent banner is currently necessary: STRAGTA does not set analytics, advertising or preference cookies and does not use browser local storage or session storage.

Technical delivery

The hosting and infrastructure providers may process ordinary server request data, such as IP address, browser information and timestamps, to deliver and secure the service. This does not cause STRAGTA to place information on your device.

Contact form and PACO

The contact form sends data only when you submit it. PACO uses an in-memory random session identifier and sends data only after you tick the acknowledgement and send a message. Neither feature stores an identifier in your browser.

External links

If you follow a link to a social network or another website, that service may use cookies under its own policy. This statement will be updated before STRAGTA introduces any optional cookies or similar tracking technology.
